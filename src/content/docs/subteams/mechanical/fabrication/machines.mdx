---
title: Machines
---

Fabrication is based on using machines to make parts.

## Subtractive

These machines subtract or take away material to create the desired part.

### Lathe

The large, spinning drill that you use knives to cut hex shafts on.
### Nebula

A CNC __router__ that uses an endmill to cut polycarbonate or wood.

### Omio

A CNC __router__ that uses an endmill to cut aluminum.

### Tormach

A CNC __mill__ that uses a large variety of different endmills to cut steel or aluminum.

#### Mill vs Router

Mills are larger than routers. Mills are more heavy-duty, and specialize in cutting harder metals than routers. 

## Additive

These machines add or eject material to create the desired part.

### 3D Printer

An additive machine that adds material instead of cutting, in order to create the desired design.
